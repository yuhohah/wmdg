# Glossary

**A Esfera**
The central deity and playable entity in *Cult of the Sphere*: a Hermetic sphere whose center is everywhere and whose circumference is nowhere. Clicking the Esfera inside the central Relicário (`Altar da Esfera`) channels **Fé** directly from mortal minds.

**Fiel Devoto**
The cult's base generator of passive **Fé**. Devotees can be recruited individually or together through **Converter Máximo**.

**Pedido de Milagre (Miracle Plea)**
A temporary plea from a devotee that rewards direct attention with **Fé**. Its reward follows the cult’s production and click power, independently of the stored Fé balance.

**Selos Sagrados**
The sequential thresholds that open Encarnação, Ritos de Fervor, Relíquias and Gnose. The first three consume Fé; the Selo da Gnose recognizes the ascension threshold without consuming it.

**Encarnação Sagrada (Avatar)**
The divine manifestation that evolves from Neófito through Consagrado, Ancestral, Eclipse and Solar, strengthening Fervor and the Fiéis. Avatar Solar requires Gnosis Suprema.

**Bênção 2× Fé**
A temporary blessing charged through the Altar da Encarnação that doubles passive Fé. Vigília Perpétua keeps this blessing active permanently.

**Fervor & Ritos de Fervor**
Fervor is the cult’s sacred fire, which strengthens Fé and funds Ritos. Ritos develop Fervor, clicks, Fiéis and Fragmentos; spending the fire preserves the production strength already earned during the current ascension.

**Fragmentos & Relíquias (Opus Magnum)**
Fragmentos are the remains of fallen divinities, obtained through Transmutação and passive production and spent restoring Relíquias. Spending Fragmentos preserves the Arca’s production strength already earned during the current ascension.

**Gnose & Árvore Hermética**
Gnose is the permanent currency earned through Ascensão Gnóstica and spent on the twenty nodes of the Árvore Hermética. Assimilated nodes strengthen production permanently; guardians, achievements and these nodes survive later ascensions.

**Automações Gnósticas**
Permanent purchases that independently automate recruitment, Ritos and Relic restoration, each with its own pause control. Recruitment reserves Fé for the chosen objective; Olho do Infinito makes automated recruitment and Ritos free and enables passive Transmutação.

**Automação dos Selos**
Ruptura Automática reopens eligible Selos after ascensions; Memória Hermética separately preserves Encarnação and enables bulk purchases. Cripta Eterna preserves restored Relic levels.

**Pico do ciclo**
The highest available balance of Fervor or Fragmentos reached in the current ascension, retained when spending that resource. Both peaks disappear at Ascensão, including when Cripta Eterna preserves Relic levels.

**Objetivo do culto**
The next Selo, Avatar or amount of Gnose the player wants to reach. Automation weighs recruitment against saving for this objective, and the player may change it at any time.

**Ressonância dos nós**
The permanent production strength granted by every assimilated Gnose node, in addition to its individual effect. It rewards purchases rather than repeated ascensions.
